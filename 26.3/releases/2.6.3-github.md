# Village Quest 2.6.3 — Living Kingdoms

There are more stories to find between villages, and clearer ways to follow the adventures already waiting at home. This update brings small roadside quests, independent story journeys and a broad pass over quest guidance and village life.

## New features

- Meet wandering travelers and Nightrunners in the Overworld. Their occasional personal quests ask for provisions, a little company, a village greeting, hunting help, iron or lights along the road. Read the task and reward in a compact conversation window, then follow it in your Journal and tracker.
- Every roadside quest pays 4 emeralds and 2 experience levels, with one rewarded completion per quest-calendar day. Accept deliveries before gathering supplies; the full bundle is handed over automatically once you have it, wherever you are. An unfinished walk can continue with another Wanderer.
- Follow Core and Living Kingdoms stories independently, including separate party choices and chapter progress. Your Journal keeps their tasks distinct alongside local stories, commissions, special quests and shared projects.
- Use the confirmed personal `/vq reset` to begin your own adventure again. It preserves other players' progress, ordinary belongings, shared contributions and existing reward limits. Pending transactions must be settled first; read the confirmation carefully.
- Begin local village help at the Notice Post without first registering a trade route. Build your village bond through practical requests and qualifying local trades, with clearer steps toward village authority and the Council Seat.
- Take Peaceful alternatives for combat Dailys, Weeklies and Marius's watch quests. Pilgrim and story instructions explain their exact supplies, destinations and next steps.

## Bug fixes and improvements

- Keep full quest rules available after acceptance. English, German and Spanish instructions now distinguish collecting, crafting, delivering, waiting and returning; material trackers show independent requirements together.
- Fix the Journal crash caused by an inn's additional summary line (GitHub #10). Keep unrelated interactions, such as boarding a minecart outside the Overworld, out of the caravan route warning path.
- Recognize fence tables topped by pressure plates, closed horizontal trapdoors or wool carpets in inns and council buildings. Keep inn opening progress and its explanation clear of the scan buttons.
- Let travelers retry blocked walks and shorter safe paths after pauses. Refine danger reactions and retain their bounded visits, combat limits and ordinary spawn caps.
- Remember party members' names while they are offline instead of displaying UUID fragments. Preserve saved story and party progress, reward choices and delivery evidence across chapter changes and reconnects.
- Make Cartographer's Lens searches and home travel safer, preserve unloaded encounter enemies until their outcome is known, and protect existing Starreach cache locations and contents.
- Explain service prerequisites, local standing, reparation and market requirements more clearly. Fix premature or misleading reward previews while retaining existing payout receipts.
