**Village Quest 2.6 — Living Kingdoms**

Your villages have a new story to tell. Our biggest update yet brings memorable people, thriving markets and new adventures to the roads you already know—with beautiful medieval menus to guide the journey.

## New features

- **Build a community.** Name your settlement, furnish a council, inn, market and storehouse, and earn five stages of village recognition.
- **Meet your neighbours.** Talk to village staff and rare inn guests. Follow Simon's travels, chart Lutte's coastal signals and stand watch with Marius for unique rewards.
- **Open the market.** Welcome up to five visiting traders, discover regional goods and connect villages through real routes, supply promises and friendship meetings.
- **Leave your mark.** Earn the Village Seal, face meaningful diplomatic choices and follow The Unwritten Promise with your hard-earned guild tools.
- **Choose your rhythm.** Adjust world options in the Journal, including optional Minecraft-day quest resets, and enjoy clearer maps, objectives and quieter world news.

## Bug fixes and improvements

Refreshed interfaces, clearer English/German/Spanish text, safer trading and rewards, and more reliable village, guest and quest progress. Existing progression carries forward.
