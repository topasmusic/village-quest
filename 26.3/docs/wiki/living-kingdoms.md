# Living Kingdoms — 2.6

Living Kingdoms is Village Quest **2.6.2 for Minecraft 26.3**.

## Where to start

1. Claim your first ordinary Daily reward at the Questmaster.
2. Accept **A Place at the Table**. Build and confirm a Council Seat, then name
   your settlement. An empty settlement can be registered before it is inhabited.
3. Claim chapter one to unlock the Inn Barrel, Market Desk, Storehouse Anchor
   and Trader Stall recipes. Confirm those buildings and assign a trader place.
4. Meet the village staff, complete real market trade and freight work, then
   earn the first village project. Finish the story to earn your **Village Seal**.
5. Develop the village's five recognition levels, market visits and direct
   agreements. After **Bells of Concord** and the required personal tools, follow
   **The Unwritten Promise**.

## Passing travelers

Ambient travelers are temporary encounters. They walk short safe routes, pause
briefly, and reply to an interaction according to local conditions. A blocked
walk now leads to a finite pause and shorter attempts in other directions, rather
than a permanent standstill. A safe partial route can still be used when a wall
or ledge blocks its far end. They react to visible active monsters rather than
calm neutral mobs or enemies hidden behind walls. Each listener has separate
speech pacing; repeated suppressed clicks no longer interrupt travel. These
changes preserve finite encounter lifetimes and combat/search limits.

## Journal and village map

The Journal's village overview shows recorded buildings, known contacts, routes
and recognition. A registered building is not a fresh construction approval.
Unknown data remains unknown; remote views never inspect distant chunks.
Village reports include last-known life state, adult count and observation age,
four official building states, and route/recovery guidance. “At least 64” is the
observation cap. **+**, **~** and **!** distinguish active, recovering and abandoned
villages; tooltips explicitly say **Last recorded**.

Open the village map from **Journal → Villages/Network → Map**, **Council →
Relations → Village map**, or the private staff information links. Village cards
open paged reports of supply, buildings, direct known agreements and saved trips.
The map shows up to forty agreements per village and refers you to the council
for additional agreements. Without trade access it is read-only. Escape or X
returns from a report to the map; Journal returns to the book, and the map's X
closes to play. Known terrain keeps its ground preview while details are painted;
unexplored terrain remains unexplored.

No page, map preview, conversation or agreement creates items, currency, residents
or completed quests. Actual server-confirmed service events determine progress.

### Market service geometry

A usable counter has two opposite reachable standing spaces at the counter's
height, giving the merchant and customer separate sides. It needs a separate
chest or barrel within three horizontal blocks (Manhattan distance) and one
block of height. An outdoor Vanilla counter uses two adjacent surfaces; an
actual Market Stall can provide a single surface. Floor blocks and storage
across the market cannot supply this evidence. Light and the clear handover
space at the market anchor are still required. New trader places require a
Market Stall block; valid saved Vanilla counter places retain their compatibility.

### Trust and council requirements

Trust shows your score against the next actual rank: 0/20, then 20/60. The next rank and remaining trust are shown together. Respected starts at 60; the score can still rise to 100 without another rank. Negative standings show the next recovery rank.

A council mandate uses the separate local village bond, not Guild trust. The council names the required bond (Trusted); earn that bond through the village Guild Notice Post before requesting the mandate.
