# Living Kingdoms Stories and Tools

Part of [Living Kingdoms 2.6](living-kingdoms.md).

## A Place at the Table

Available after claiming your first ordinary Daily reward. It is separate from
the older village-core arcs.

| Chapter | Actual progression |
|---|---|
| 1 | Accept to learn Council Seat crafting. Confirm/register a furnished council and choose the village name; claim for the other three anchor recipes and Trader Stall recipe. |
| 2 | Confirm the inn, market and storehouse in any order, and assign at least one trader place. Existing confirmed buildings can count. |
| 3 | Speak to the leader, innkeeper and storekeeper at valid local buildings. |
| 4 | Complete a paid confirmed market trade and actual storehouse freight delivery. |
| 5 | Earn the first village recognition project and return to claim your personal Village Seal. |

The village target stays fixed when you build elsewhere. At a Council Seat use
**Choose as story target** to deliberately recover/change it; earned chapters
remain. Only the party leader may replace an existing shared target. Admitted
offline members receive the same change. Party membership grants no village
management mandate. See [Buildings](kingdom-buildings.md) for the separate empty
settlement and inhabited-recognition requirements.

In **2.6.2-unreleased.1**, the Journal's next-step card names the first unfinished
requirement of the current chapter, including trader-place assignment, each staff
conversation, paid trade, freight and recognition. When all chapter requirements
are earned it directs you to the Questmaster to claim. The original objective
list remains below it. These hints read recorded evidence without remotely
inspecting a building or granting progress.

## Village Seal

Finish **A Place at the Table** to earn the **Village Seal** (Village Check tool).
Use it near registered buildings: up to eight official anchors within 48 blocks
receive ten-second markers. Green means ready, angry particles mean missing
furnishings, pale particles mean incomplete evidence. Private chat lists locations,
statuses and missing criteria; the private diagnosis explains quantities and
assignment conflicts. Only loaded fresh evidence counts. If a check is pending,
stay nearby and try again after the five-second cooldown.

The tool never loads chunks, builds structures or grants extra project rewards.
Use the **Guild Archive** if it is lost. The first replacement is free; later
replacements use the archive cost and daily cooldown. A replacement invalidates
earlier copies, including stored ones. Creative/borrowed seals grant no personal
rights. A party member completing the story while offline can obtain the earned
seal from the archive.

## The Unwritten Promise

The personal postgame story opens after the actual **Bells of Concord** finale
claim, completion of **A Place at the Table**, and personal eligibility for all
nine permanent guild tools plus the Village Seal: Starreach Ring, Merchant Seal,
Shepherd Flute, Apiarist's Smoker, Surveyor's Compass, Caravan Ledger, Roadwarden
Horn, Wayfarer's Sigil and Guild Courier's Satchel. Borrowed/Creative items do not
replace earned rights. Lost tools remain recoverable from the Guild Archive.
Party members can use the same places with their own entry rights, effects,
choices and rewards.

The five scenes retrace a supply promise copied from a travel note without a
sender or recipient:

1. Trace an actual village connection with the Compass, upgraded Ledger and Sigil.
2. Guide a real animal with the Flute and fill a hive with the Smoker.
3. Inspect a functioning building with the Village Seal and operate a high lever
   using the Ring's extra two-block offhand reach.
4. Change a Pilgrim or Wandering Trader offer with the Merchant Seal, pack a real
   guild contract in the Satchel and supply/complete that exact contract.
5. Use the Horn, confirm a new route arrival and choose the reserve or open-road
   charter at the selected council.

Choose a destination on one of your real direct village-to-village routes. The
recorded village-story target is offered first. Near a single fresh verified
council you may represent, `/vq artifacts target` explicitly selects a new target,
including between chapters. Earned chapters stay; the current scene needs new
actions. Local farm/signal/inspection actions must be within 96 blocks of the
recorded council.

Animal guidance needs a successful real path, at least four blocks of movement
from an initial distance of six blocks, and safe arrival within three blocks.
The Ring must toggle an allowed lever beyond normal reach; Creative and protected
or cancelled clicks do not count. Existing valid buildings count. No tools are
handed over, old farms damaged, or raids/ceremonies created by the story.

Pack the Satchel with the accepted contract's full cargo, including at the sending
village, then supply/finish that same contract normally. Cancelling/reaccepting
creates another identity. There is no extra freight reward. Busy/daily-limit
Merchant responses, an already full hive or a Flute sound without animal arrival
do not prove their objectives. If an encounter/target becomes unavailable, a new
local target/scene attempt is possible. A Horn already used that day can count
through real active-incident orientation; otherwise wait for its ordinary reset.

For the last choice, use `/vq artifacts reserve` or `/vq artifacts road` at the
selected council. Reserve recommends the large building package; road recommends
rails. Both keep all reward choices, save a personal title and Chronicle entry,
and reduce the Village Seal cooldown from five to three seconds. Existing reward
escrow protects a full inventory. After completion these commands can revise the
recorded charter/Journal epilogue without paying again.
