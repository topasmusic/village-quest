# Village Quest 2.2.0 - The Shrines Between Roads

The roads now remember the villages they connect. Restore forgotten shrines, earn each settlement's trust, and turn the Caravan Ledger into a living Guild Atlas.

## New features

- Continue the trade-route endgame through the six-chapter `The Shrines Between Roads` story after opening the Caravan Yard.
- Discover five persistent village identities—Granary, Forge, Pasture, Apiary, and Archive—with twenty rotating local delivery requests.
- Improve each village from Known to Trusted and Allied over multiple daily resets, unlocking better local Wayshrine prices and cooldowns.
- Build and bind two-block Guild Wayshrines, choose destinations from a terrain-backed map, store shared Magic-Shard charges, and let guests use the same physical network.
- Explore the journal's new Guild Atlas: a painted Guild Path, eight Charter landmarks, and a static five-track Trust roster with authoritative progression.
- Recover lost unique guild tools through the server-authoritative Guild Archive; superseded recovered copies remain inert.
- Earn and use the Wayfarer's Sigil, Cartographer's Lens upgrade, restored shrine cores, and optional Guild Courier's Satchel.
- Craft the Guild Wayshrine, Guild Notice Post, Emberglass Lantern, and Guild Milestone from the dedicated Village Quest recipe-book category.
- Encounter three new shrine-network route incidents while caravans continue to travel and complete freight normally.

## Bug fixes and improvements

- Rebuilt the Guild Notice Board as a localized illustrated interface with exact inventory progress, rewards, daily availability, bond thresholds, and atomic item hand-in.
- Added four request variants per village identity, fivefold delivery quantities, difficulty-scaled rewards, and deterministic rotation without immediate repeats.
- Hardened Wayshrine travel against forged targets, unsafe arrivals, combat, active route incidents, missing endpoints, insufficient funds, and unauthorized rename or break actions.
- Made the Broken Heartstone ruin choose conservative natural wilderness, avoid inhabited or constructed terrain, sink cleanly into the ground, and preserve unresolved targets across abandonment.
- Added safe recovery and invalidation rules for unique tools, temporary story items, and renewable replacement shrine cores.
- Unified the Guild Atlas and Journal frame, removed bright edge seams, improved long-text placement, and made every Village Quest screen respect the configured inventory key.
- Added a first-open Atlas tutorial, stable progression mist, clearer Charter silhouettes, readable Trust emblems, and responsive hover details.
- Fixed the server-config startup log's unsupported formatting placeholders.
- Completed and validated all release text in English, German, and Spanish.

Available only for Minecraft `26.2`, Fabric Loader `0.19.3` or newer, Fabric API `0.153.0+26.2` or newer, and Java `25` or newer.

Minecraft `26.1.2` and `1.21.11` remain maintenance lines and do not receive the 2.2 content package.
