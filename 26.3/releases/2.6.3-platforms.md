# Village Quest 2.6.3 — Living Kingdoms

Take the long road home: a Wanderer may need provisions, a Nightrunner could use a hand, and your village still has stories of its own.

- **Small roadside stories:** discover six personal quests in compact conversation windows, with clear rewards and automatic completion. Earn 4 emeralds and 2 experience levels, once per quest-calendar day.
- **Room for two journeys:** follow Core and Living Kingdoms stories independently, on your own or with your party.
- **A clearer fresh start:** begin again with a confirmed personal reset while keeping other players' progress and existing reward limits intact.
- **Practical village help:** take local Notice Post requests before registering a trade route, and follow clearer guidance toward village trust and authority.
- **Better directions:** read complete quest rules, track material requirements together and use explicit supply alternatives on Peaceful.

This update also fixes the inn-related Journal crash, improves traveler movement, remembers offline party names and recognizes more fence-table combinations.

Back up your world before updating and choose the download matching your Minecraft version.

**Minecraft 26.3 · Fabric Loader 0.19.5+ · Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 · Java 25.** Install Village Quest and Fabric API on both client and server, with the same Village Quest version.
