# Founding, Buildings and Village Projects

Part of [Living Kingdoms 2.6](living-kingdoms.md).

## Four official buildings

| Building anchor | Role |
|---|---|
| Council Seat | Register/name the settlement, manage rights, recognition, relationships and diplomacy. |
| Inn Barrel | Register the inn, its innkeeper and certified free bed. |
| Market Desk | Register the market area and explicitly assign up to five trader places. |
| Storehouse Anchor | Register the storehouse and its storekeeper; inspect actual freight history. |

Accept **A Place at the Table** after your first Daily reward for the Council Seat
recipe. Claiming its first chapter grants the other three anchor recipes and the
Trader Stall recipe together. See [Stories and tools](kingdom-stories-and-tools.md).

The unlocked shaped recipes each produce one anchor; use the recipe book for
their arrangement:

| Anchor | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Council Seat | 5 planks of any wood, 1 book, 2 sticks. |
| Inn Barrel | 1 barrel, 2 paper, 2 sticks. |
| Market Desk | 3 planks of any wood, 4 sticks. |
| Storehouse Anchor | 1 chest, 1 paper, 3 sticks. |

Place the anchor and read its building checklist. Hover a requirement for its
accepted blocks and arrangement; click it for found/required/missing quantities,
full placement help and detected marker targets. A valid building needs loaded,
safe, connected evidence. The range control explains its X/Y/Z scan dimensions
and horizontal/vertical reach. Normal range is 17×17×9; extended is 25×25×17.

All five story chapters now explain their next steps in game. For a generated
village's mandate, complete local requests at its Guild Notice Post until the
permanent village bond is **Trusted**, then return to the Council Seat. The
Journal also opens Trust and shows village bonds. Unlocked recipes appear in the
general crafting search as well as the Village Quest tab.

If a ceiling lies outside the small area, nearby furnishings remain detectable;
extend the area to include that roof. A roof is still required for council/inn
approval, and furniture in a separate building across an uncovered yard cannot
qualify the council.

Counted seats are dry, straight bottom-half stairs with their open side facing a
counted table 1–3 blocks away on the same line and at most one block difference
in height. An inn bed needs matching foot and head halves. Market storage needs
one container; a storehouse needs four. Double chests count once. Loose profession
blocks do not assign Vanilla jobs to villagers.

## Settlement versus inhabited recognition

A furnished valid council can register and name an **empty settlement**. You do
not need villagers beside every anchor. The first council in a generated village
saves the selected name and building together. Existing names and identities stay.

To earn inhabited-village recognition for the first project, the registered area
needs **three adults, three distinct assigned reachable beds, and at least one
matching assigned active reachable workplace**. Only actual loaded Vanilla
assignments count. Recognition is earned once and survives residents moving or
dying; current population and current building validity remain separate.

Abandoned villages begin recovery with two adults and two assigned reachable beds.
Full activation requires three adults, three such beds and the confirmed loaded
recovery period. Losing a resident or bed pauses recovery; only repeatedly
confirmed complete depopulation abandons a village. Active villages do not have
to repeat a new founding threshold. Unloading proves neither loss nor recovery.

## Registration, repairs and names

Open an inn, market or storehouse after its check. A uniquely connected village
is detected from the checked area; a nearby disconnected village is insufficient.
Select explicitly only where several village territories meet. Establish a council
nearby or extend the checked area when there is no valid connection.

The screen shows the saved village and registration status. Repairs and pending
checks preserve membership. **Check again** refreshes evidence. A range change
uses **Apply area change**; **Relocate building here** preserves the logical
building identity. Management still requires the village's mandate.

Choose a name when founding or use **Name** at a council you manage. Names allow
1–48 characters including spaces and accents; control/formatting codes are
rejected. Enter saves; Escape cancels the draft. Routine updates preserve the
draft and focus. Losing permission or selecting another village cancels it.
The name is shared by the Journal, trade map, Notice Board and dialogue without
changing identity, routes, reputation or earned progress.

## Village staff

Each official council, inn and storehouse has its own persistent named speaker,
innkeeper or storekeeper. They appear only on certified safe loaded floors near
players. Unload and relocation preserve the logical person. A genuine death needs
an actively observed day before return. Inn/storehouse staff can remain at valid
buildings in an abandoned village. Promotion changes clothing while keeping the
same speaker's face. Caravan appearance also belongs to the saved crew member.

Innkeepers use Minecraft sleep/wake and ground navigation at a certified free inn
bed. The lease survives unloading/sleep and protects existing villager homes.
They approach a certified floor beside the bed at night and return to the inn
front during the day. Storekeepers patrol a small certified area and carry a
torch at night. Visible nearby hostiles can make staff seek a safe certified
inn/council area and warn nearby players; they do not fight or ring the bell.
An actual failed safe-path search gives a waiting message. Unknown/unloaded
evidence or a spent path budget does not assert that the path is blocked.

The innkeeper reports traders actually at usable places, including a real name
and home. Scheduled arrivals, unloaded bodies and closed visits do not count as
present traders. The storekeeper describes your recorded direct connections and
actual deliveries. Road condition is last recorded; inspect the ledger/map to
investigate it. These conversations add no goods or extra service rewards.

## Five recognition projects

At the Council Seat open **Village projects** for the next project's evidence.
Only a manager can confirm it. Levels belong to the village and persist through
temporary building or population trouble. Their requirements are:

| Project | Required real evidence |
|---|---|
| 1 | Inhabited recognition; council, inn, market and storehouse; three staff conversations; paid market trade; supplied freight. |
| 2 | Four valid buildings; paid trade and freight; two usable trader places; an active visit. |
| 3 | Council; three usable places; an active direct trade/supply agreement; completed leader meeting. |
| 4 | Council; four usable places; confirmed promised crisis aid. |
| 5 | Four valid buildings; five usable places; signed agreements with two different partners; a fresh assigned-workplace check. |

Earned recognition permits one to five visitors in a later regular market visit.
It never enlarges or refills the current visit. See [Markets](kingdom-market.md)
and [Diplomacy](kingdom-diplomacy.md).

## Village Check

The earned **Village Seal** inspects nearby official anchors; it does not build or
approve them remotely. The private diagnosis separates found, ready, missing and
checking buildings, with actual missing counts and assignment conflicts. Its
open view refreshes for 30 seconds; use the tool again for another observation.
A closed view stays closed. See [Village Seal](kingdom-stories-and-tools.md#village-seal).


For the fifth project, use **Check assigned workplace** at the council, then
confirm while its fresh local proof is current. Staff conversations must actually
finish at valid buildings. Paid trade and supplied freight count for the player
who performs them; borrowed contacts and old trips do not replace that work.
