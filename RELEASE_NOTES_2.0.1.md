# Village Quest 2.0.1 - Interface Frame Hotfix

This focused visual hotfix cleans up two interfaces from the Village Quest 2.0 update.

## Fixed

- The Quest Board now shows its complete wood and brass frame. Its right and bottom edges are no longer cut off.
- The Pilgrim Trader no longer has transparent gaps between the parchment and the upper-left or left wood frame.
- The Pilgrim footer text is centered cleanly inside the wooden bar, with more space beside the left corner.

No quests, progression, economy, caravans, or save data were changed in this update.
