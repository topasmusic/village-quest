# Diplomacy, Village Initiative and Crises

Part of [Living Kingdoms 2.6](living-kingdoms.md).

## Relationships and agreements

At an official Council Seat open **Relationships** for your known canonical
contacts. Four village cards fit on each list page; the network view shows the
same selected village with named, colour-coded trade/supply/friendship links.
Hover for full names and explanations. Stored freight, help, disagreements,
loyalty breaks, reconciliation and the next repair step remain distinct.
Unknown villages and indirect alliances are not revealed.

Choose a partner, **trade**, **supply** or **friendship**, and qualitative terms.
Both village mandates are required. A player managing both villages can represent
each at its own council. Counteroffers replace earlier consent. The other leader
can agree or explain preferred terms; real shortage takes priority over ordinary
preferences. Replies appear privately in chat and in the panel, with full hover
text. Signing does not construct a route or pay currency.

## Supply and direct routes

The panel shows the actual direct route and its pause/incident/resurvey reason.
Two connections through a third village and a personal Caravan Yard are not a
direct village-to-village route. Economic effects require the real verified link.

A supply proposal names the requesting village's recorded need. Accept an
appropriate Trade Guild freight contract, deliver matching goods **to that
village** on the functioning direct route, then accept the current offer with its
current revision and the other mandate. Ordinary arrivals, wrong goods, opposite
direction and replayed receipts do not count. The board prioritizes pending
promises within existing rank/daily limits, including coal, leather, honeycomb
and paper cargo. There is no extra freight payout. Real deliveries build shared
pair history even outside the player's current story target.

## Friendship meetings

After both representatives approve friendship, invite the other leader from the
host council. The same named person travels virtually and appears only on a fresh,
safe loaded council floor near players. Confirm while both leaders are awake,
visible and close on that floor. Cancellation allows another invitation; unload
preserves the invitation. Confirming signs the pact and lets the guest go home.
The third village project uses a completed meeting and an active direct trade or
supply agreement as separate evidence.

## Village initiative

Regular real freight over an active direct route can build a trading habit.
Saved active direct connections and reliable known needs now progress while an
authorized player is online elsewhere, including with unloaded village chunks.
A bounded scheduler checks at most eight active councils per second. Restart,
missing observation gaps over one minute and extra observers add no catch-up. Several
reliable active days and at least three real deliveries may lead to a small
autonomous trade agreement; cautious leaders take longer. Quiet active days soften
the habit without cancelling permanent agreements. No goods or rewards are added.

Leaders may privately request supply/friendship mediation. Speak to the leader or
read the stored cause in Relationships, then use ordinary offers and consent.
New requests join the quiet world-news report even when you are away.
The request signs nothing and cannot bypass existing proposals or loyalty cases.
A cleared shortage retires a supply invitation without penalties for silence.
Known partners with active direct routes are considered first; beyond 32 eligible
partners, bounded observation windows rotate. A busy network may develop slower.

## Shortage mediation

A signed supply partner with a reliable shortage may request **Mediation**. Hear
both existing leaders. Promising aid requires their conversations and new matching
consumed freight from home to the partner. Competing home/partner needs are stated.
No cargo, money, enemies or personal aid credit are invented.
Requests can be created from reliable saved reports while you are elsewhere;
decision time still advances only in the safe local context below.

**Keep supplies home** explicitly pauses only this pair's supply promise. Visit
the disappointed leader, acknowledge the decision at the council and hear both
leaders again. Repair follows the recorded character: a new real friendship
meeting, improved reliable supply or matching freight after apology, or a working
direct route. Earlier meetings/deliveries do not replace the new repair. Practical
freight remains an alternative when another player owns the shared need report.
Reopening creates fresh negotiations, mandates and consent.

## Loyalty: The last full wagon

When home and a known partner both have recorded shortages under signed supply
and friendship promises, the council can ask for a rare loyalty choice. Hear both
leaders. Promising help requires real matching freight. Explicitly choosing home
pauses supply **and breaks friendship**; the panel explains this before selection.
Deferral, elapsed time, offline time and improved conditions are neutral.

Reconciliation needs new conversations and the disappointed leader's actual repair
task. A repair meeting does not renew the pact. Reopening requires fresh supply
and friendship proposals, ordinary consent and an official meeting. Practical
road repair also needs new freight after apology. Other village pairs remain.

## Conscious refusal of caravan aid

A real loaded outbound ambush may privately offer help/refusal buttons, also
available as `/vq aid help <incident>` and `/vq aid refuse <incident>`. The owner
must be healthy, equipped, out of combat and near the visible living caravan and
attackers, with a verified dry approach and enough rescue time. Unclear situations
give no warning; silence is neutral.

Three different freshly confirmed refusals create personal distrust at the route's
supply village and block new friendship negotiations there. Existing founder
rights and other villages remain. Agree to help and resolve a new real ambush,
or use `/vq aid repair` beside the disappointed leader at a fresh valid council
and deliver new matching freight for the saved need. The freight alternative
works in Peaceful. Earlier cargo/wrong destinations count for neither repair,
and no second payout is added.


If a reply seems stuck, close and reopen the council to inspect its saved state.
Decision timers pause while you are away or unsafe; silence is never refusal.
