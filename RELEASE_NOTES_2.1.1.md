# Village Quest 2.1.1 - Homesteads & Wayfinding

Your own base can finally become part of the village trade network—and the road beyond it is clearer, steadier, and far more configurable.

## New features

- Turn a safe player-built base into a Homestead Trade Post as soon as the Market Charter unlocks the Caravan Ledger, while destinations remain real inhabited vanilla or CTOV villages.
- Survey ocean routes with proper ferry legs, safe shore boarding points, dashed sea lanes, boat markers, and live arrival times. Lakes and rivers still require roads or bridges.
- Keep one stable illustrated map across panning and sessions through deterministic per-world terrain tiles stored in a bounded cache.
- Configure reset timezone, caravan visibility, map quality, HUD/minimap layout, notifications, sounds, markers, and tutorial hints without changing save progress.
- Choose Full, Reduced, or Map only physical caravans while the virtual route economy keeps running normally.
- Use the read-only `/vq diagnose [player]` report to inspect routes, homes, incidents, road quality, traders, and possible stuck or orphaned caravans.
- Toggle the Quest Tracker with `.` and the minimap with `,`, both grouped under `Village Quest` in Controls.
- Enjoy a unified visual pass for all eight active Village Quest tools and relics, including a bespoke Caravan Ledger, Roadwarden Horn, and 32-frame Surveyor's Compass.

## Bug fixes and improvements

- Increased Restless Pens horseback travel to 4,800 blocks and removed the repetitive sound from every distance tick.
- Rebalanced Bakehouse Help so the required wheat naturally covers both bread crafting and the final delivery.
- Added clear Trust milestone bars and corrected when Prosperity becomes available.
- Made map terrain, shading, shorelines, and decorative marks remain visually stable while panning.
- Added player markers to both maps and made all route-map layers respect their visibility settings.
- Made Daily and Weekly resets timezone- and daylight-saving-safe for the actual server location.
- Shortened special-item tooltips, restored the Journal Collection icon, and removed verified unreachable legacy resources.

Available for Minecraft 26.2, 26.1.2, and 1.21.11.
