# Village Quest 2.0 - Roads Between Villages

Village Quest 2.0 turns the roads beyond your first settlement into a long-term village network.

Available for Minecraft `1.21.11`, `26.1.2`, and `26.2`. Existing Village Quest worlds are supported; no save wipe is required.

## Build real trade routes

Register inhabited villages, survey up to five routes with your own waypoint detours, improve the roads, and watch route-colored merchant caravans travel through the world. The new terrain map and configurable live minimap show villages, the player, caravans, incidents, and the paths you actually built.

## Grow the Village Trade Guild

Complete freight contracts, choose route specializations, install permanent upgrades, resolve road incidents, and climb five Guild ranks. Route distance, road quality, lighting, security, daily limits, and offline escrow now shape the economy instead of rewarding only the shortest straight line.

## Continue the road story

`The Empty Caravan` is a new six-chapter follow-up to `Shadows on the Trade Road`, leading through an abandoned wreck, a trail of clues, village witnesses, a player choice, and a bait-caravan defense. Completing it establishes the permanent `Caravan Yard` and opens the full route network.

## A unified Village Quest interface

The Journal, Questmaster, Pilgrim, Caravan Ledger, Route Office, full map, and minimap now share one compact Minecraft-style dark-oak, parchment, brass, and teal design. Quest pages are easier to scan, tooltips wrap correctly, route states are clearer, and Pilgrim rotations are capped at four readable offers.

## Progression that lasts

Experience rewards now remain useful at both low and very high vanilla levels. The update also adds reputation Mastery, daily rerolls, the Roadwarden Horn, improved relic loops, rebalanced quest/shop rewards, route investments, and safe one-time backfills for existing players who already qualify for new unlock items.

## Compatibility and reliability

Caravans recover more naturally from rough terrain, paused or removed routes clean up their physical members, abandoned villages are rejected, CTOV village structures are recognized through the shared village tag, and farming quests support compatible right-click-and-replant harvest mods without double counting normal harvests.
