# Quest Reference

This page is a compact reference for the current visible quest pools and story chapters.

## Current Reward Rules

- Easy, standard, and hard Daily wallet rewards start at `3`, `6`, and `12 Silvermarks`, then follow the stored light/normal/heavy profile.
- Quest experience is paid in bounded level bars instead of a fixed raw-XP amount. A `+3` reward advances the same three bars at vanilla level `10` and level `200`; vanilla determines the larger raw-XP cost at the higher level.
- Normal Daily difficulties grant `1.5`, `3`, and `4.5` bars. Normal Weeklies grant `5.25`, `6`, or `6.75`; Pilgrim combat tiers grant `3`, `4.5`, or `6`; Story rewards scale with their chapter weight and cap at `7.5` bars.
- Light and heavy repeatable profiles still lower or raise the matching bar reward. Fractional payouts are rounded to quarter bars, and the unlocked Forge Charter adds a separate `+0.75` bar to Crafting rewards.
- `/vq daily reroll` replaces an unaccepted Daily once per reset day; three combined reputation Mastery levels raise that to two.
- `Fresh Finery for Your Companions!` requires a tuned sequence of successful collar recolors instead of one interaction.
- Each normal Pilgrim combat roll presents an Overworld-safe contract plus a second alternative, preventing Nether/End-only dead days.
- Story projects apply their new reward bonus consistently to the chapter that completes the project.
- Pure action-only Daily and Weekly quests complete automatically on their final objective.
- Item-consuming Daily and Weekly quests, every Story chapter, and every Special commission require an explicit `Questmaster` claim. Delivery bundles are revalidated and consumed only during that hand-in.
- Quest verbs are literal: `mine`, `harvest`, `craft`, `smelt`, `breed`, and similar actions must happen after acceptance; `bring`, `deliver`, `provide`, and `turn in` check the items carried at claim time. Hybrid quests require both parts.
- Pure deliveries accept stored or traded goods unless their text also names a fresh action. Because Minecraft merges item stacks, fresh work is stored as quest progress while the final bundle is checked separately; pre-existing stock never replaces the fresh action itself.
- Locked Quest Board entries place an `Unlock requirements` section directly below their status. Completed prerequisites are green, missing prerequisites are red, and relic commissions show both their required completed Story and current Reputation progress. Daily and Weekly reset timers appear only on their matching tabs, never in the Special/Relic tab.
- The tracked-quests HUD colors a numeric objective green once every `current/target` pair on that line is complete.
- Mining objectives use the exact item stack generated by the broken block, including Fortune-sized ore drops. Pre-existing stock still does not count toward requirements that explicitly ask for resources gathered after accepting the quest, while the final hand-in separately verifies the items currently carried.
- Quests with a real production dependency now advance one stage at a time and show only the current step. Later work is credited only after the prerequisite stage is complete: `Bakehouse Help` harvests before baking, `Meal from the River` catches before cooking, `Smelting for the Smithy` mines before smelting, `Harvest for the Village` finishes all three crops before baking, and `Smith's Week` finishes both raw ores before smelting. The final delivery remains a separate Questmaster hand-in.
- `Bakehouse Help` calculates its fresh-harvest stage as `wheat delivery target + (bread target × 3)`. The extra grain is exactly what the required loaves consume, so a player following the stages can still carry the intended wheat-and-bread bundle to the Questmaster.

## Normal Daily Pool

These are the daily quests currently rolled by the normal `Questmaster` daily system:

### Farming / Food

- `The Queen's Gift`
- `Bakehouse Help`
- `Kitchen Supplies`
- `Meal from the River`
- `Autumn Harvest`

Ripe wheat, carrot, and potato objectives accept both normal block harvesting and compatible right-click-and-replant harvesting. Village Quest verifies that the mature plant was actually reset or replaced before granting progress, so an ordinary right-click, an immature crop, or an unsuccessful bone-meal use does not count. `Kitchen Supplies`, `Bakehouse Help`, `Harvest for the Village`, and `The Failing Harvest: Thin Fields` credit the full collected crop-item stack rather than one point per plant; this includes multi-item Potato and Carrot yields without counting the same harvest twice.

### Crafting

- `Fuel for the Workshop`
- `A Favor for the Smithy`
- `Smelting for the Smithy`

### Animals

- `Fresh Finery for Your Companions!`
- `Wool for the Weaver`
- `New Life in the Stable`

`Fresh Finery for Your Companions!` uses the same light/normal/heavy target tuning as other repeatables; every counted step must be a real successful wolf or cat collar recolor.

### Village / Trade

- `Business in the Village`
- `Market Rounds`

## Randomization Summary

- `Daily`: random from the normal non-combat daily pool
- `Weekly`: random from the normal non-combat weekly pool
- `Pilgrim` dangerous rumor: random `1` per day after `Watch Bell`
- `Roadmarks for the Compass`: fixed one-time special override, not part of the random rotation

## Availability Feedback

- New Daily, Weekly, and Story offers receive a single localized Questmaster chat announcement.
- Daily and Weekly notices use their reset day or week as the persistent identity. Story notices use the exact arc and chapter, including the chapter revealed after a cooldown.
- `The Questmaster Listens` is an informational Story entry: it remains openable, shows the one-hour countdown, and deliberately contributes `0` to the category badge.
- Quest audio follows one hierarchy: acceptance, ordinary progress, completed objective, stage transition, then the existing final reward sound.
