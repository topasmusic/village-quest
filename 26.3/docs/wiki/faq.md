# Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I continue my world?** Yes. Existing progression is retained. Back up the
world before updating and use the same Village Quest version on client/server.

**Where do I begin?** `/vq questmaster`, one ordinary Daily, then claim its reward.
Open the Journal for next steps. [Getting Started](getting-started.md).

**Why is Story or Special locked?** Story needs your first normal Daily reward;
Special needs your first real reputation gain. Later entries have their own gates.

**Do I need villagers before founding?** A furnished valid council can register
an empty settlement. The first recognition project separately needs three adults,
three assigned reachable beds and a matching assigned reachable workplace.
[Building guide](kingdom-buildings.md).

**Can I connect a village I built myself?** In 2.6 for Minecraft 26.3, found it
through a confirmed Council Seat and meet its normal inhabited/active-village
requirements. Register it with the Caravan Ledger inside the confirmed area;
Guild Notice Posts use that same settlement identity. Route unlocks still apply.
[Trade routes](trade-routes-and-caravans.md).

**Why is the inn quiet?** It has a 40 loaded-minute opening period. Rare guests
also need a populated managed village and a suitable inn; they are occasional,
not guaranteed. [Guests](travelling-npcs.md).

**Why is there no trader?** Confirm the market, assign a usable trader place and
allow the visit to arrive. Recognition limits the next visit to one–five traders.
Reopening a market does not refill stock. [Markets](kingdom-market.md).

**Where do quest items come from?** Fresh-action objectives need new work after
acceptance. Delivery objectives use the matching items carried at hand-in.
Keep complete bundles out of storage when claiming.

**My inventory was full—where is my gift?** Free space and use `/vq rewards claim`.
Do not repeat a purchase just to recover pending goods.

**I lost an earned tool.** Use the Guild Archive. Personal earned rights, replacement
costs and cooldowns still apply; borrowed tools do not replace progression.

**Can friends share everything?** Supported quests use party sessions; tools,
guest quests and some story choices remain personal. Party membership grants no
village management rights. [Progression](core-progression.md).

**Can I change timers or hide speech?** Open `/vq settings`. Owners/operators set
world options; everyone can set their own tracker, minimap, news, sounds and bubbles.
[Configuration](configuration.md).

**Which versions have Living Kingdoms?** Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3 share the
2.6.1 content release with native APIs. Other versions retain their published
releases and may receive scoped maintenance.
[Support policy](../../../VERSION_SUPPORT.md).

**How do I report a problem?** Include Minecraft/mod versions, a clear reproduction
and relevant logs. `/vq diagnose` provides a read-only report.
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/topasmusic/village-quest/issues).
